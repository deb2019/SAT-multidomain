Reproduce from this directory: g++ -O3 simulate.cpp -o simulate && python run.py && python temporal_random.py && python summarise.py
Inputs are verified previous pilot counts (1,790 controlled batches on 37 datasets plus 26 temporal batches across four case studies; bank overlaps). N = pool size, D = number of disagreements, K = positives in D. Source and SAT cutoffs are fixed before querying labels. FP cost 1, FN cost 5. No target outcome is used to construct a proposal. All policies keep source on ties and skip audits when D=0. Distinct labels capped at 10, 25 or 50 per batch.
Reference: Sawade C, Landwehr N, Scheffer T. Active Comparison of Prediction Models. NeurIPS 2012, equations 3, 5, 12, 13, Algorithm 1. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2012/file/92fb0c6d1758261f10d052e6e2c1123c-Paper.pdf
Our exact policy samples uniformly without replacement in D. At budget m, x~Hypergeom(D,K,m). One-sided exact alpha=.05 against zero or negative cost saving. For a lower challenger cutoff, test K<=floor(D/6); for a higher cutoff, test K>=ceil(D/6). empirical_same_sample draws the same way but chooses the lower observed cost. This isolates decision-rule effects. Random_exact samples uniformly from the whole pool using the same exact decision rule conditional on sampled disagreements.
AMC_cost_hard is a cost-sensitive hard-decision instantiation of Theorem 1, not the paper's numerical zero-one-loss experiment. Use the thresholded classifiers as point-mass predictive distributions in Eq. 13 (tie of the two votes on disagreement). With sign s=+1 for lowering, -1 for raising, gain is s*(5 for a positive or -1 for a negative) on D. Estimated average gain delta=s*2D/N. For each agreement the per-item proposal weight is |delta|; within D it is sqrt(((5s-delta)^2+(-s-delta)^2)/2). Self-normalised importance sampling chooses the empirical lower-risk policy. Since gain is zero on agreements and weights equal within D, sign of gain estimate follows signed draw counts within D. Sample with replacement, remembering deterministic labels and continuing to the distinct-label cap; repeated draws contribute to the importance estimate. Three strata pooled for efficiency; group sampling and new-vs-seen probabilities are identical to item-level draws. 4,000 replications/batch; common paths across AMC variants and budgets. AMC_Wald_5pct applies a one-sided normal critical value 1.64485, keeping source when estimated standard error is zero. AMC_Wald_limit also accepts strictly positive estimates with zero standard error. These two deployment gates are additional variants; the original paper reports a two-sided p-value and returns the empirical winner. Informative per-record probability versions of the proposal were not evaluated. Counts alone do not contain that information.
Full_gain: expected gain over the source across the realised batch. Remaining_gain: sample-by-sample gain on the complement of the distinct labelled items when the switch is accepted, zero otherwise. The complement differs by sampler. Neither includes annotation expense or the direct benefit of manual decisions. Temporal gains sum within case; avoid pooling cost units across domains. Remaining gain/labels is a break-even price in false-positive-equivalent units under the source-reference accounting used here. Negative signifies no positive label-price break-even. Not a currency or forecast. Not a comparison with a comparator which also has free labels. Controlled cost = equal-weight mean of dataset means. Beneficial/harmful approvals are conditional batch means across 1,214 beneficial or 397 harmful batches (179 ties excluded). Batches share source splits; 4,000 Monte Carlo draws are numerical repetitions, not independent experimental datasets. Monte Carlo standard error for any individual switch probability <=0.008. Exact policy probabilities and gains use finite-population sums.
